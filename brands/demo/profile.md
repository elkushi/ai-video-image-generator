# Margin Notes: demo profile (fictional brand)

Last updated: 2026-10-26. A made-up brand for test runs and the smoke test. It is not a real company or product.

## What it is
A fictional note-taking habit channel. It posts short tips on writing things down, planning a week and keeping a simple notebook.
Type: service

## Proof points (approved numbers)
- None. This is a demo kit, so videos must not state numbers or results.

## Products / features to feature
- Weekly planning page: one page, five minutes, every Monday.
- Margin method: write the question in the margin, the answer in the body.

## Audience
Students and early-career professionals who scroll Instagram and LinkedIn and want simple habits that take a few minutes.

## Tone
Calm, plain, practical. Say: "Write the question first." Never say: "This will change your life."

## CTA defaults
- Educational: "Save this for Monday."
- Promo: "Follow for one tip a week."

## Never
- Invented numbers, testimonials or clients
- Real people's likeness, political topics

- `logo/monogram-white.svg`: for dark and brand-colour backgrounds
